Symbolic monograms

The letters M and K form the symbol itself: joined into one mark, with engineering (gear) and cooling (ice) built in.

Option S1 · MK ligature

M and K share one stroke, so the two letters lock together as one mark. Navy M for engineering, green K for cooling.

Option S2 · Frost MK

The same joined MK, but the arms of the K are ice-crystal branches: half the letter is a snowflake.

Option S3 · Gear + MK

A gear (engineering) holding the joined MK in green and ice blue (cooling). The whole name in one symbol.

Option S4 · Frost gear badge

A solid gear in a green-to-ice gradient, with the frosted MK cut out in white. Strongest as an app icon, sticker or van logo.
